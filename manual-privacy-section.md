## 🔒 Privacy & Children's Data
`👩‍👧 For Parents`

ChorePal is designed to be used by children under 13, so a parent is always in control of what is saved about their child.

**What a parent agrees to:**
1. When you tap **+ Add Kid**, you are asked to confirm you are the child's parent or guardian and agree to the Privacy Policy before the profile can be created.
2. ChorePal saves the child's name or nickname, avatar, PIN, chore history and the chore photos they submit. It does not ask for the child's age or email address.
3. Read the full policy any time in **Settings → Privacy Policy**.

**How photos are handled:**
- The photo is checked by Google's Gemini AI. If ChorePal does not verify it, it is never saved.
- A verified photo is deleted as soon as you approve or reject the chore (or after 30 days if nobody reviews it).
- About 90 days after a chore is reviewed, the child's name, avatar and your comment are erased from that record.

**Deleting data:**
- Tap a child on the dashboard, then **Delete Child Account**, to permanently delete their profile and everything saved about them.
- **Settings → Delete Account** deletes your account and all of your children's information.
